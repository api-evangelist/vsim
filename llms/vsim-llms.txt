# Vsim

> Vsim (Vsim Technology Ltd) is a Manchester, UK physics-simulation startup founded in 2022 by ex-NVIDIA simulation engineers Michelle Lu (CEO) and Kier Storey (CTO). It builds a next-generation physics simulation platform and an end-to-end reinforcement-learning training framework for robotics, reporting up to 100x training speed-ups over existing solutions. Backed by a $21.5M seed round led by EQT Ventures (total ~$24M). Vsim does not currently publish a public API, developer portal, or SDKs.
